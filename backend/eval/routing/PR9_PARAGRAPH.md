**Intent routing: benchmark, measured fix, experiment** (commits `3e00bc5`, `6b67373`, `3c50e06`). A new routing benchmark (`backend/eval/routing/`, 232 labelled single messages: every assertion in `test_phase2_intent.py` and `test_multi_agent_orchestration.py`, the ten misroutes from the conversation eval, the six audit sentences, 20 new Indian-English patient openings per agent, 8 intake guards) scores `IntentClassifier.classify_detailed()` and the orchestrator's final routing separately and attributes every miss to a rule. Baseline on this branch: classifier 186/230 (81%), orchestrator 195/230 (85%). The misses fell into five groups: the `I am / I'm` introduction regex (7), medication keyword order via the generic words safe/schedule/timing/warning (6), overbroad single keywords (5), the intake prototypes semantically pulling any "knee" sentence (11), and short messages falling through the semantic thresholds to the default agent (15). The fix in `lam/intent_classifier.py` only: those five words out of the medication list, `I am` no longer an introduction without an intake cue, whole-word matching in the medication follow-up check, and no semantic INTAKE_CONTEXT without an intake cue; keyword-vs-semantic order and thresholds untouched. Result: classifier 200/230 (87%), orchestrator 202/230 (88%), no agent below its baseline at either step (Recovery 23→28, Rehab 23→26, Pain 27→29, Daily Activity 19→21, Mental 15→17, others unchanged). Conversation eval through the orchestrator: 271/306 properties (270), 10 misrouted turns (11); "Am I on track with my knee?" now reaches Recovery. `run_agent_tests.py` 109 passed, orchestration 5 passed, `test_medication_proactive.py` 21 passed; `test_phase2_intent.py` keeps 18 pre-existing failures in its forced-fallback sections, identical on the unmodified classifier (they expect a fallback path for messages that hit a keyword rule first). The semantic-first variant (Sentence-BERT first, keywords as tiebreaker) is behind `SEMANTIC_FIRST` / `LAM_SEMANTIC_FIRST=1`, off by default: 198/230 classifier (Recovery −2, Rehab −1, Wound −1, Mental +2), 207/230 orchestrator lenient but 182/230 strict, and it breaks the medication→wound handoff test, so it is not recommended as is. Full before/after per agent, every miss and its rule, and what remains: `backend/eval/routing/REPORT.md`.
