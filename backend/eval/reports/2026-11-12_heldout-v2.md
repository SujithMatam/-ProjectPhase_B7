All 9 messages without a medication cue (H009-H017) go to Medication in the classifier, for the same reason. A symptom word that main added to `_MEDICATION_KEYWORDS` (`itchy`, `itching`, `bloated`, `dizzy`, `light-headed`, `constipated`, `nauseous`, `fatigued`) wins, because the medication rule is checked first. The orchestrator's lenient score hides most of this, because `detect_applicable_intents` also dispatches the right agent next to Medication. The strict score (23/30) shows it.
