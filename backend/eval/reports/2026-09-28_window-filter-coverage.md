## Summary

- **Wrong-window chunks:** 19 in default mode, 12 with the window filter, out of 34 chunks retrieved in each mode.
- **Coverage:** with the window filter, 7 queries are fully covered (default: 6), 2 partly (p02, p08) and 1 not at all (p04). p09 moves from partial to full. No query got worse.
- **Limit of the filter:** it can only reorder the 8 nearest candidates within the 0.75 threshold. p02 still has 3 wrong-window chunks because only one matching chunk was in its top 8, and the chunk that answers the question (EV-THA-PAIN-01-c1) was not among them.
- **Boundary days:** the match is exact on day numbers. At day 21 (p06), a days 22-42 chunk counts as wrong-window.
- **Coverage gap (from the corpus audit):** window labels written as weeks convert to `days` inconsistently, so days 43-55 have no stage-specific entry for any procedure and domain. At, say, day 49, the window filter can only prefer the broad entries: PAIN-06 (1-84), TKA-REHAB-05 (8-84), THA-REHAB-02 (1-56) and REC-05 (22-84). See `2026-09-28_corpus-audit.md`.
