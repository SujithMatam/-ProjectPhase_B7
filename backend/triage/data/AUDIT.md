# Audit of the original triage dataset

Generated by `backend/triage/data/audit_source.py` from the files in `source/` (see `PROVENANCE.md`). Normalisation replaces `Left`/`Right` with `{limb}` and `day N` / `Post-Op Day N` with `{day}`.

## 1. Rows vs distinct sentences

| split | rows | distinct raw messages | distinct after normalising |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| train | 420 | 82 | 63 |
| val | 90 | 49 | 47 |
| test | 90 | 55 | 51 |
| **all** | **600** | **88** | **63** |

Every normalised sentence is repeated on average 9.5 times (min 4, max 14). The generator (`dataset_builder.py`) has 22 scenarios x 3 templates = 66 templates; 63 of them appear in the data.

## 2. Overlap between splits (normalised sentences)

| pair | shared sentences | share of the second split's distinct sentences | rows in second split whose sentence is also in the first |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| train / test | 51 | 51/51 (100%) | 90/90 (100%) |
| train / val | 47 | 47/47 (100%) | 90/90 (100%) |
| val / test | 38 | 38/51 (75%) | 65/90 (72%) |

Test sentences never seen in train: 0.

The original splits are therefore a memorisation test, not a generalisation test.

- Rows with temperature >= 38.5: 29, all RED, all from 1 family (Purulent foul discharge with systemic fe...). It is the only family ever given a fever.
- Rows with 37.8 <= temperature < 38.5: 0. The generator meant to give the "Low-Grade Temperature" (YELLOW) family 37.8-38.2, but tests `'Fever' in domain`, which is false, so that family got 36.5-37.2 like everything else.
- Every other row (the other 6 RED families included: DVT, PE, neurovascular, bleeding, compartment, dislocation) has 36.5-37.2.
- So `temperature_c` >= 38.5 is a perfect RED leak for one family and the field carries no information otherwise; the fever-question family has a normal stored temperature while its text talks about fever.
- Rows whose message states a temperature that differs from `temperature_c`: 17 (e.g. "Feeling a little flushed and feverish this evening, thermometer says 38.0°C." stored as 36.6).
